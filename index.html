Common questions

What happens on the 15-minute call?

Mostly you talking and me asking questions, and by the end you know whether I can help and what I'd do first. No pitch, because I'd rather you leave with something useful than a quote.

Which service should I start with?

If you're a company, book a quick chat and we'll work out whether it's training, fractional CTO work or advisory, because most teams aren't sure and that's what the call is for. If you're an engineer and your resume needs work, start with Fresh Squeeze. If you want ongoing support across a few areas, Career Accelerator is the best value. If you have one specific challenge, book a Focused Hour.

What happens after I pay?

For Fresh Squeeze, you'll get instructions on how to submit your resume. For Career Accelerator and Focused Hour, I'll follow up to schedule and send a pre-work intake so we hit the ground running. Company engagements don't go through checkout at all. We agree the scope on the call, I invoice through Learn with O.J. LLC and we start with a small well-defined project before either side commits to more.

Can you guarantee a promotion?

No. Anyone who says they can is selling something. What I can do is show you how the decision actually gets made at your company and get you doing the things that make it a no-brainer.

Can I upgrade from Fresh Squeeze to Career Accelerator?

Yes. If you start with a resume review and want to keep going with sessions, just let me know. I'll work with you to make the transition seamless.

Who is this best suited for?

Engineering leaders whose team is working with AI and nobody's sure what's safe to ship, or who have a product in prod with nobody senior owning the technical calls. On the other side, experienced software and infrastructure engineers who haven't gotten the senior title yet, and senior engineers who want peer support on a big initiative. If you're stuck or want clarity on what it actually takes to clear the next bar, this is for you.

How do engagements with companies work?

Direct billing at $325 an hour through Learn with O.J. LLC, with training and retainers packaged around deliverables rather than billed by the hour. I'm an independent contractor, 1099 in the US, and your team keeps full control of priorities and IP. I work alongside your existing team in your tools and your meetings, and when something sits outside software and infrastructure I bring in a vetted specialist who bills you directly.

Can my company pay for this?

Yes. Many engineers use professional development budgets or learning stipends. There's an email you can send your manager and I can invoice your company directly if that's easier.

What's the refund policy?

If we can't find a time that works or circumstances change before we meet, I'll refund you in full. After the session, I stand behind the value delivered.
