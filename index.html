Common questions

Which service should I start with?

If you're not sure, reach out on LinkedIn and we'll figure it out together. If your resume needs work, start with Fresh Squeeze. If you want ongoing support across multiple areas, Career Accelerator is the best value. If you have a specific challenge and just need one focused conversation, book a Focused Hour.

What happens after I pay?

For Fresh Squeeze, you'll receive instructions on how to submit your resume. For Career Accelerator and Focused Hour, I'll follow up to schedule your session(s) and send a pre-work intake so we can hit the ground running.

Can I upgrade from Fresh Squeeze to Career Accelerator?

Yes. If you start with a resume review and want to keep going with sessions, just let me know. I'll work with you to make the transition seamless.

Who is this best suited for?

Experienced software and infrastructure engineers who haven't gotten the senior title yet. Also senior engineers looking for peer support with challenges or leading larger initiatives. If you're feeling stuck or want clarity on what it actually takes to clear that bar, this is for you.

Can my company pay for this?

Yes. Many engineers use professional development budgets or learning stipends. I can provide an invoice for your company if needed.

What's the refund policy?

If we can't find a time that works or circumstances change before we meet, I'll refund you in full. After the session, I stand behind the value delivered.

The bigger picture

These services are the first step toward something larger. I'm building a guided learning platform for engineers. The goal is to make senior-level knowledge accessible to everyone.

Every dollar goes directly toward building that platform. When you work with me, you're investing in your growth and helping make quality engineering education accessible to others.
